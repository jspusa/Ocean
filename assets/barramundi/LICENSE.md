The original geometry was converted to the game's coordinate frame, without
reducing triangles or replacing its UVs, normals, or tangent handedness. Texture
maps were downsampled with correct colour/data semantics. The original asset has
no animation; the game's bounded swimming deformation is separately authored.
The asset's metre dimensions are not a measured biological specimen.

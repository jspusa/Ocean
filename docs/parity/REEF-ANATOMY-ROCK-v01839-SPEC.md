# Continuous reef anatomy and shallow-rock surface response

This is the next implementation within the unchanged user goal: believable underwater animals, lighting and rock strata; real physical contact; varied, populated habitats; grounded coral; automatic desktop/mobile quality; direct verified publication. v0.18.38 is the baseline (`c10b3e1`). The previous goal turn made verified progress through that public release. Full commercial or photoreal readiness is not established.

## Observed visual defects

Actual v0.18.38 fixed-world images under `qa/parity/v01838-materials/` show four newer reef fish with ellipsoid bodies, separate bulb-like mouths/peduncles/eyes and flat angular fin panels. Banggai's main body and fin outline and Fire goby's protruding white mouth remain visibly assembled even after pigment continuity was fixed. Longnose's snout is a tube attached to the body. Near shallow rocks in the turtle-oblique and public touch entry image have broad smooth green/clay-like surfaces; existing pigment already contains crust, and a second procedural green layer overwhelms bare rock. Existing depth-based AO should not be duplicated.

## Required result

1. Replace the primary skin of Anthias, Banggai, longnose and Fire goby with one closed continuous anatomical surface from mouth/head through trunk and caudal peduncle, differentiated by species profiles. Remove redundant pigment spot solids. A main-skin connectedness/manifold witness must fail against the actual old production meshes, then pass on both generated and compiled new LODs. Dark mouth/gill marks may be shallow distinct details; they must not become protruding substitute balls.
2. Curved thin fin membranes share coherent normals and edges rather than independently extruded flat panels. Fin roots remain seated in the actual new body. Preserve recognizable fork/rounded tails, separate cardinalfish dorsals, long Fire goby first dorsal and forceps-like longnose. Eyes should sit in the head, with a restrained iris/pupil instead of a pale external ball. Review actual side, front, oblique, far and lamp views on desktop and touch; numerical geometry checks alone do not prove the artwork.
3. Preserve species order, UID/genes/actor counts/routes, physical scale, saved progress, shared instancing and high/low LOD coverage. Keep the existing authored/high-detail x extents and therefore pigment registration (1.047, 1.279, 1.025). Both new LODs explicitly sample those shared endpoints; the old odd-ring low-detail ellipsoids slightly missed the equator, so eliminating that low-LOD scale drift is an intentional correction, not an assertion that old low vertices stay unchanged. Tags remain in existing body/fin/eye ranges; use existing tail/median fin sub-tags where appropriate. Keep the eight-model aggregate indexed geometry budget at or below the measured old budget. No extra draws, shaders or per-frame geometry work.
4. Shallow rock material 2 should retain recognizable bare limestone, localized living crust and upward sediment without a second uniform green overlay. Combine both normal-map layers from the original geometric normal and one shared set of projection weights, then normalize once. Preserve geometry and existing rock contact/substrate data; do not add an AO pass, texture assets, raymarching or per-frame allocations. Verify actual near/far/oblique and lamp views, all production shader compilation and bounded rendering cost. A surface-response improvement does not resolve the still-simple giant rock silhouettes.
5. Rebuild the real compiled/streamed geometry after fish changes. Test actual transformed fish vertices against the production collision envelope, affected reef identity/paths, material registration, and normal gameplay entry. Preserve audio-independent loading, explicit pointer/touch labels and automatic quality. Native GPU checks remain serialized by root; touch emulation is not a physical-phone or battery result.

## Release acceptance

Retain visual baseline and valid mesh RED; produce actual new compiled views and independent art review. Run affected existing geometry, material, contact and identity checks. Inspect all changed compiled resources and record performance limitations honestly. Complete independent Standards and Spec review before committing; after authorized publication verify Pages commit, every manifest file, ordinary desktop/touch entry and audio regression. Do not claim the broad realism goal complete from this release.

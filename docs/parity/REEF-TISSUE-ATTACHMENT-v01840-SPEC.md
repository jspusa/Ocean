# Continuous reef tissue and actual substrate attachment

This continues the full, unchanged realism/contact/ecology/automatic-quality goal after the publicly verified v0.18.39 baseline `cffbc44761ed53b1f1af58a3da7a13cf40bdef57`. The prior goal turn made authoritative progress through implementation and verified publication. This work must not be mistaken for complete photoreal or commercial acceptance.

## Observed defects and causal evidence

The real v0.18.39 fish views show a dense screen-aligned fin grid. Fresh touch-profile captures record actual specialized-fish `uSamples=2` and renderer MSAA=2. A diagnostic changing only coverage for the four newer reef species to 1 removes the grid at unchanged geometry, camera, resolution, lighting and MSAA. This is not baked vertex color; actual Anthias fin vertices have a constant color. All material-6 fins share this coverage branch, including other real fish kinds, so the correction must not preserve the same defect on an arbitrary kind boundary. Existing thin-tissue backlighting remains an approximation; full optical transparency is not claimed.

The real compiled plate colonies have three separate bowl-like plates and three independent cylindrical feet. The actual triangle audit covers 19 families/962 instances. For 123 plate colonies, 191 of 369 foot footprints across 98 colonies are strictly above all supporting high-rock/compiled-terrain triangles. The strong node-1214 third-foot witness has at least 0.29776 m clearance even after clipping the entire footprint against substrate triangles. Correcting only the center height leaves a 0.12694 m gap. Raw evidence is in `qa/parity/v01840-realism/coral-root-audit.{cjs,json}`. Other families' minimum-vertex diagnostics are not whole-foot certificates.

## Required behavior

1. Remove artificial fin sample holes for every actual material-6 fin while preserving fin pigment/RGB, existing backlighting and smooth silhouette antialiasing. Do not disable MSAA globally or alter other material coverage, LOD fades, creature identity, animation, save, pointer/touch labels or automatic quality. Actual emitted-alpha RED/GREEN covers real compiled body/fin attributes at 0/2/4 samples; synthetic fixture transforms are explicitly disclosed. Actual world near/far/lamp desktop/touch views remain necessary. No new pass, framebuffer, texture or per-frame allocation.
2. Replace both existing foliose color variants with distinct continuous basal tissue and irregular, curled, overlapping lobes. Remove the three-cylinder/three-disc construction. Both use one closed, consistently oriented connected skin with finite unit normals and a real path from basal attachment to every lamina. All high/middle/far LODs sample the same shape and root and retain recognizable silhouette. Numerical topology is not art acceptance. Do not increase colony counts, consume RNG, or duplicate per-instance full crowns. After the rejected low-budget prototypes, resource allocation may change between shared LOD meshes only with explicit archive/stream residency and complete-world timing evidence; the original per-mesh caps are not an art acceptance substitute. The measured fourth-form authoring envelope is 7,440 / 2,984 / 1,476 triangles per foliose variant and 28,734 / 4,548 / 1,860 for the shared fan; these are limits to audit, not proof of acceptable frame cost.
3. Replace the obvious sea-fan Voronoi card appearance with tapered curved main branches, finer branches and irregular real connecting branches at bounded high/low resource cost. Do not retain a visually dominating polygonal membrane merely to satisfy old tests. Preserve shared batching, holdfast and gentle rooted motion; the two currently static hero fans need the same intended fan movement. The same 2 hero and 9 cliff instances may gain middle/far LODs through four additional batch headers, sharing exactly three fan geometry/indexed objects. Their default ranges are continuous at 0–5.5 / 5.5–20 / 20–220 m; any shorter support-dependent visibility must be derived and recorded, not a blanket hiding threshold. No new actors are authorized by this change. Actual oblique/back/near/far views must show the open structure.
4. Attach the actual continuous basal region/holdfast to actual visible rock, terrain or wreck substrate. Replace nearest-cell rock-height estimation where used for these placements with cached actual-triangle queries; retain the established contact module and exact anemone support behavior. Wreck placements remain exact except the two explicit pillar-clearance translations below. Grounding must use the real root geometry, not assume instance origin is the bottom. No blanket downward offset may substitute for a demonstrated attachment. A geometric witness must reject genuinely detached roots, and compiled world checks must cover the resulting instances/LODs. Keep horizontal ecological placements/counts, identity and player/fish rock contact unchanged unless a measured required correction is explicitly documented.
5. Rebuild actual archive/streamed resources and review all changed batches, nodes and derived metadata. Verify primary anatomy, contact, habitat, identity, original entry/audio behavior and natural automatic-quality swimming. Root serializes native GPU tests; no other tests run during cost measurements. Physical phone/battery results remain unproven without actual devices.
6. Placement search runs offline. Browser construction replays the versioned, source-bound receipt without performing that search. The compiler records exact affected node identities; replay validates every input before changing any actor or visibility range. Source, archive and replay changes during packing must reject before the assembled HTML/config outputs are first replaced and before a successful manifest/build result is declared. Scene partitioning currently writes chunks/index before the later snapshot check; failed packing can therefore leave those local derived files changed. Such a failed build must never be published, and a fresh verified build is required. This is a mixed-input rejection guard, not atomic publication or rollback of every derived file. Cross-engine pose arithmetic must use a documented precision established from actual Node/WebKit measurements, while changes to real geometry, source, counts, family, scale and support remain detectable. A source-hash mismatch cannot silently invoke the expensive fitting search during entry.

The bounded attachment correction may move only actors that fail actual root/crown/support checks: at most 3.3 m horizontally and 2.5 m vertically from their recorded source poses, with yaw/pitch changes only to fit a measured local support facet. Every such change must retain actor identity and scale and carry reason/support evidence in the compiled replay. The test oracle must reject `unresolved` contacts; a failed bounded certificate is not itself proof that a colony is completely floating. Root support checks include all potentially co-visible substrate LODs and actual quality distance scales.

Three measured legacy cliff placements are the sole explicit exception to the 3.3 m horizontal search limit. The first integrated solve left foliose-0 near `[-8.89,-31.78,-36.9]`, fan near `[-7.89,-31.88,-36.3]`, and fan near `[14.43,-30.62,-51.3]` with supported roots but buried/intersecting crowns. Dense bounded candidate searches failed; this is not a proof that every continuous pose is impossible. Captured actual-neighborhood candidates require 4.123–4.505 m horizontal displacement and at most 2.319 m vertical displacement. Their complete original identities and exact final poses must be enumerated in the source, independently checked, and referenced by a unique authored-relocation reason in the replay. Only these three exact entries may use horizontal <=4.6 m; the vertical limit stays 2.5 m, all other targets retain 3.3/2.5, and the same actual root/crown gates remain mandatory. No global relaxed bound or arbitrary actor exception is permitted.

The complete compiled crown oracle additionally found two genuine intersections between the broader wreck foliose leaves and existing deck pillars. Only original nodes 2742 and 2746 may translate by the exact 0.35 m and 0.40 m source-authored deck corrections in `WRECK_PLATE_CLEARANCE_RELOCATIONS`. Their complete original identities and final positions are pinned; height, yaw, pitch, scale, genes and tint stay unchanged, as do the other 18 wreck foliose/fan actors. These authored source placements remain outside the 596-actor search replay and require both actual deck-root and crown clearance proofs. They may not be hidden by relaxing the crown predicate or exempting the whole wreck.

Native fallback acceptance must use the actual Renderer as well as recording constructors. If streamed and archived models are both unavailable, load the same authored coral forms used by the compiler before generation; otherwise the legacy generator consumes a different random sequence and invalidates the attachment replay. Load those forms only on this fallback path, with a bounded request/body and explicit recoverable error. The portable build includes their data URL. Release temporary source-form and fan construction caches after upload, including the aquarium's fan cache, while retaining uploaded GPU batches. The compressed full-archive bound is 26 MiB for the measured approximately 25.3 MB candidate; decoded data remains bounded at 80 MiB. Test all three real entry routes separately.

The larger irregular-rock silhouette work remains part of the same objective. It depends on reliable attachments: `rockGeometry` also builds rubble and giant islands, so a shallow-looking change can alter 835 contact instances. The current verified floating-foot issue must be resolved coherently before using a silhouette tweak to hide it.

## Release acceptance

Use actual mesh RED/GREEN plus native controlled and normal-play evidence; independent Standards and Spec review before commit. Publication is already authorized. Verify exact Pages build, all public manifest files, ordinary desktop/touch entry and current strict audio entry after publishing. Disclose quality/cost tradeoffs, procedural limitations and untouched realism work. The full goal remains active until all original requirements are actually established.

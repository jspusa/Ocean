# v0.18.37 work scope within the continuing realism goal

The full active goal remains believable underwater lighting, rock and animal surfaces, physically coherent contact, variable life beyond the central reef, grounded coral, automatic desktop/mobile quality and verified public publication. No slice proves commercial readiness or Wii superiority.

## Current implementation and acceptance

1. Player, first/third-person camera, observation ray and swimming animals must respect visible rock and arch surfaces, including rotated/nonuniform instances, shelves, overlaps and small/large moves. The surface data must survive compiled/streamed/fallback loading and GPU LOD eviction. Exact actual triangle witnesses, real input journeys and bounded CPU candidate work are required. Existing ellipsoid differential tests only preserve fallback behavior, not the new visible-surface contract.
2. Turtle dorsal and ventral skins meet coherently, light plastron is externally visible, head/neck is continuous and fin tips closed at both LODs. Shell/head/plastron stay rigid during strokes; fin roots stay seated; animated normals follow transformed tangents. Actual geometry and native GPU tests plus independent rendered views are required.
3. Anemone and wreck coral roots must attach to actual substrate triangles, with paired clownfish habitat following the anemone. Do not lower already-attached colonies indiscriminately. Validate all affected colonies in generated/compiled output and visible views.
4. Fresh dives should offer ecologically bounded encounter variation outside the central reef while preserving individual identity, photo references and resumed-dive continuity. Per-frame random relocation is forbidden. This requirement remains open until implemented and validated through the actual save/start/reload flow.
5. Keep automatic quality, audio-independent boot, pointer/touch-only labels and saved progress. Performance validation must include actual natural movement and shared contact queries; preserve truthful desktop/touch-emulation versus physical-device boundaries.

Remaining goal work beyond this slice includes the other visibly simplistic fish, broader lighting/material assessment, full habitat journeys, physical-device performance and content completeness. These remain explicit open requirements; do not mark the whole goal complete from this release.
